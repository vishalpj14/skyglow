Some things about a sunset are exact. Others are educated guesses. Skyglow keeps the guesses in a small settings file called a **pack**, so they can be improved without changing the app.

## What is exact, and what is a guess

**Exact:** where the sun is, when it rises and sets, and when Earth's shadow reaches a cloud at a given height. That is geometry.

**Guesses:** everything a pack holds:

- **Where each stage starts and ends**: for example, "afterglow is when the sun is 2° to 4° below the horizon".
- **Typical cloud heights**: low, middle and high cloud sit around 1.5 km, 5 km and 9 km in many places, but higher in the tropics.
- **Colour names and swatches**: which colour to expect for light that has dived to a given height.

## Why packs exist

A sunset in Kerala is not a sunset in Iceland. Tropical clouds sit higher, the air is wetter, and twilight is shorter. A **regional pack** adjusts the guesses for one part of the world, while the exact maths stays the same everywhere.

Skyglow comes with two:

- **default**: a sensible starting point for anywhere.
- **kerala**: higher tropical clouds and lower cloud bases, for the south-west coast of India.

Choose one under **Date, clock and pack**, or load your own pack file.

## How packs get better

Every logged sunset is a test: did the pink arrive when the pack said it would? With enough real observations, the guesses can be tuned and a new pack released. The app picks it up without needing to change.

## Honest note

The colour guesses in today's packs are based on very few observations. They are a starting point, not a promise.
